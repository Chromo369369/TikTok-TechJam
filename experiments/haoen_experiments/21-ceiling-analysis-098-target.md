# Where this leaves the 0.98 target

0.9565 is a real, validated gain over the 0.929 hand-tuned baseline -- the closest this project has come to the 0.98 target -- but reaching 0.98 itself still requires getting very close to this scenario mix's actual ceiling, not just improving on the margin. At Hit Rate@10 = MRR = 1.0, hitting a technical score of 0.98 requires MTTC ≈ 2.16; at HR = 0.99, it requires MTTC below 2.0. Structural floors push the other way: Intent Override sessions (15% of the mix) cannot register a hit before their forced override turn (3 or 4) by construction, and Boundary sessions (5%) always burn turn 1 on a scripted rebuff — a back-of-envelope floor from those alone puts minimum achievable MTTC around 1.9, meaning 0.98 requires *essentially* rank-1, first-possible-turn resolution on nearly every one of the 200 sessions simultaneously. For calibration, the pasted "E060" report's own claimed best configuration -- built with a materially more elaborate pipeline and framed as having "closed" exposure and question tuning -- topped out at 0.9585, not 0.98; that report's own final section frames 0.98-ceiling territory as needing "a better observable item-target prior," i.e. a fundamentally stronger signal, not further tuning of the same kind. Notably, this project's 0.9565 is now closer still to that claimed-best 0.9585 -- a gap of 0.002 -- using only the disclosed evaluator mechanics and no external signal.

Confirmed dead ends: `_CONFIDENCE_GUARD_LAST_TURN` and rotation-window re-tuning (both byte-identical across the ranges swept, since neither is exercised at all on a public set this well-resolved), BM25 field-weight tuning (noise-level spread once the union recall path is accounted for), `_EXACT_HIT_MAX_FANOUT`/`_TFIDF_TOP_K` (flat -- the learned reranker adapts to either threshold rather than being sensitive to its placement), and sub-phrase indexing (a real, non-overfit signal that never once changed an outcome on the public set; reverted rather than kept as unearned complexity). Real, with one outsized surprise: the L2 re-tune plus override-gating harvest fix (+0.0084 end-to-end), the switch to a listwise objective (+0.003, plus a stability fix), `_CATEGORY_POOL_CAP` (+0.0049, the single biggest lever found in this entire project, from a constant nobody had questioned since it was first hand-picked), and feature-interaction terms (+0.0003, a genuine but small gain -- the interactions clearly picked up real signal, just not enough of it to move the end-to-end score much beyond what the base 22 features already captured). Worth remembering across every one of these rounds: an isolated ranking-gate diagnostic and the end-to-end score respond to changes at different rates (MTTC only cares about *set membership* in top-10, governed by the confidence guard independent of ordering, while the diagnostic and MRR both care about exact rank), so use the diagnostic to navigate but confirm with the real evaluator before deciding a change is worth keeping -- exactly what caught L2=150 looking best on the pointwise diagnostic while actually scoring lower end-to-end than L2=50, what caught BM25 field-weight tuning looking promising in isolation while contributing almost nothing once the union safety net was accounted for, what caught `_CATEGORY_POOL_CAP=0`'s better-looking diagnostic number hiding a genuine miss that only a direct full-10-turn check revealed, and what caught sub-phrase indexing's real, non-noise signal nonetheless never mattering for a single outcome. Every candidate from the original "fully optimized?" audit has now been tried and measured. The remaining ~0.024 gap to 0.98 looks structural at this point -- consistent with the MTTC floor analysis above and with E060's own conclusion -- rather than something sitting behind one more unexamined lever in this architecture.
